⚡ Settle Up Benchmark

Same fewest-payments math, three engines. Random table, dollars in $5 steps. ← Back to the app

The math: fewest payments = players − the most groups that can settle among themselves. Finding that means checking every subset of players, 2ⁿ of them (16.7 million at 24 players).

JavaScript is the exact code the app uses. Zig → WASM is a 1.5 KB module that processes 16 subsets at a time with SIMD. WebGPU runs one GPU thread per subset, one layer of subset size at a time.

A normal home game (≤10 players) takes microseconds on any of them.
