⚡ Settle Up Benchmark

Same fewest-payments math, two engines. Random table, dollars in $5 steps. ← Back to the app

The math: fewest payments = players − the most groups that can settle among themselves. Finding that means checking every subset of players, 2ⁿ of them (16.7 million at 24 players).

JavaScript is the app's original solver, kept here for comparison. Zig → WASM is the 1.5 KB module the app now uses; it processes 16 subsets at a time with SIMD.

A normal home game (≤10 players) takes microseconds on any of them.
